    Capture device simulator

    What the technician at a primary health centre sees. The quality gate runs on CPU in milliseconds, before any image leaves the site, so a bad capture is caught while the patient is still in the chair.

    Why this stage exists

    A CNN logit cannot tell a technician "the macula is out of frame". Nine physics-based criteria can, and they run before any heavy model touches the image. Real field failures — media opacity, miosis, lens flare, uncleaned optics — do not look like the Gaussian blur a learned quality classifier is trained on.

    One image at a time demonstrates a model.
