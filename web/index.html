      Why this beats a black-box classifier

      It shows its work

      A Grad-CAM++ heatmap plus outlined lesions — you see where the decision came from, not just a number.

      Safe by design

      A calibrated referral gate: it errs toward "refer" on sight-threatening disease rather than silently under-grading.

      Honest about limits

      It reports a quality gate and flags findings it can't assess (e.g. neovascularization) instead of faking confidence.

      RetinaScope · runs the trained pipeline in drscreen.api · not a medical device — research/education demo.
