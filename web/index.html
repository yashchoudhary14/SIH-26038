Screening Pipeline
CONDITIONING EVIDENCE DECISION
AWAITING AN IMAGE
  1. 01Capture

    The photograph arrives and is squared to its field of view, so the disc diameter can serve as the unit of measurement.

  2. 02Condition

    Landmarks fix the clinical frame. The quality gate decides whether the image can be graded, and fixes what it can.

  3. 03Extract

    Lesions are segmented at 1024 px and counted per quadrant, in the units the ICDR criteria are written in.

  4. 04Decide

    The ordinal grader and the rule engine grade independently; a threshold frozen on validation routes the case.

  5. 05Explain

    Attribution and the evidence in words are packaged as a report, and referable cases go to a person.
