    04

    The screening console, end to end.

    One photograph, four phases, one signed record. The operator at a primary health centre sees capture feedback in under a second; the reviewer sees the evidence trail that produced the referral.

    Phase 01

    Capture and gate

    Nine interpretable criteria score focus, illumination, contrast, field of view, macula presence, artefact, exposure and noise. A correctable defect is enhanced; an uncorrectable one returns capture instructions.

    Phase 02

    Locate and segment

    Optic disc and fovea fix the clinical coordinate frame, then an attention U-Net at 1024 px segments vessels and five lesion classes, fine enough to keep every microaneurysm.

    Phase 03

    Grade and decide

    A CORN ordinal head predicts cumulative severity, isotonic regression calibrates the referable probability, and a frozen threshold of 0.1999 routes the case to auto-report, referral, or a human.

    Phase 04

    Explain and record

    Grad-CAM++ over the referable log-odds, lesion evidence written in words, and the 4-2-1 rule trail. The report leaves as HTML, a PNG panel and JSON.

    06

    It sizes the programme, not just the model.

    A district screening service is a queueing network: rejected images re-enter the camera queue, bandwidth is shared and intermittent, and reviewer time is the scarce resource. The same configuration drives a SimPy reference and a Simulink SimEvents model, so every staffing and hardware choice can be tested before it is bought.

    Reviewer load from 141% to 11%

    AI-assisted triage

    Manual review asks for 141% of the reading capacity a district has, so the backlog never clears. With AI triage the same two ophthalmologists run at 11%, and the service holds at twice the demand.

    The optimiser chose AI, and chose the edge

    1,024 configurations searched

    192 were rejected analytically as unstable — every one of them an unaided-review design. The value of triage is an output of the search, not a premise of it. It also picked on-device inference over a GPU server, the opposite of the cloud-first default.

    Rs 54 per patient screened

    Cheapest feasible plan

    8 PHCs with one camera each, 1.0 ophthalmologist FTE, 50% auto-report coverage, edge inference over 3G. 100,394 screened a year at Rs 53.9 lakh, camera-bound at 54% utilisation.
